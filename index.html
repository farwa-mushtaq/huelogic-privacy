Privacy Policy

How HueLogic handles game progress and respects your privacy.

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Application ID: com.huelogic.colorsort

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to HueLogic – Water Sort Puzzle (the “App”), an offline color-sorting puzzle game. The developer or publisher identified in the App's Google Play listing is responsible for the App. If you have questions, contact us using the details in Section 9.

2. Information stored by the App

HueLogic saves gameplay information locally on your device so your experience can continue between sessions. This may include:

  • Unlocked and completed levels, stars, and puzzle progress;
  • Daily challenge completion dates and streak information;
  • Gameplay preferences such as sound, music, and vibration settings.

This information is used to run the game, restore progress, and display your achievements. Based on the described offline version, the App does not require you to create an account or submit a name or email address to play.

3. Personal data, network services, and sharing

The described offline version of HueLogic is designed to operate without uploading gameplay progress to a developer-operated server. It does not intentionally ask for your contacts, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone access as part of its core gameplay.

For the described release, the developer does not intentionally sell or share locally stored gameplay progress with third parties. Device operating systems, app stores, and services outside the App may independently process information under their own policies.

4. How information is used

Locally stored game data is used to remember your level progression, display scores and achievements, calculate daily challenge progress, and apply your audio and vibration preferences. It is not used by the described offline game for personalized advertising.

5. Audio and vibration

The App may play locally bundled sound effects, including liquid-pouring sounds, and provide optional device vibration feedback. You can adjust these options using the in-app Settings screen. These features do not require recording audio through your microphone.

6. Data storage, retention, and deletion

Game progress and preferences are stored using local application storage on your device and generally remain there until the App's data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. You can remove local game data through your device's application settings using the “Clear storage” or “Clear data” option. Uninstalling the App generally removes its local application data, although device backup or restore services may operate separately under your device settings.

As the described version does not provide accounts, there is no HueLogic account to delete. If a future version introduces accounts or cloud storage, this policy will be updated to explain applicable deletion options.

7. Children's privacy

HueLogic is a general puzzle game. The described version does not intentionally request personal information from children. If your release includes child-directed features, advertising, or external SDKs, the applicable child privacy and Google Play Families requirements must be assessed separately.

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or data handling practices change. Any revised policy will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
